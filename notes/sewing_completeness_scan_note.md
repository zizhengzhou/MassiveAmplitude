# Sewing completeness scan note

## Current verdict

The current auxiliary construction is not using one fixed auxiliary spin value.  It imposes
\[
S_a=S_b
\]
and then enumerates the allowed common value \(S_a=S_b=s\).  The value \(s\) is selected by the same on-shell construction constraints that `ConstructAmp` uses, and is then checked again after translating the auxiliary amplitude back to the formal \(J\)-slot amplitude.

For the checked four-point and five-point sectors below, the sewn records span the same reduced massless-limit vector space as `ConstructIndepCFBlock`.  In every nonempty checked case,
\[
\operatorname{rank} M_{\rm CF}
{}=
\operatorname{rank} M_{\rm sew}
{}=
\operatorname{rank} M_{\rm joined},
\]
and the independent sewing block has full row rank equal to the CF rank.

## Auxiliary spin selection

For a chosen left three-point record, let
\[
d_{\rm L}^{(0)}
\]
be the number of left brackets that do not contain a \(J\)-slot.  If the desired full sewn amplitude has bracket dimension
\[
d_{\rm amp},
\]
the right auxiliary on-shell construction is called at
\[
d_{\rm R}=d_{\rm amp}-d_{\rm L}^{(0)}.
\]
For a full \(n\)-point sewn amplitude, the right auxiliary problem has the same point count \(n\): two auxiliary massless particles plus the \(n-2\) physical particles on the right.  The code dimension passed to `ConstructAmp` is
\[
D_{\rm R}=d_{\rm R}+n.
\]

The auxiliary spin scan is
\[
s\in\left\{-n_{\rm col},-n_{\rm col}+\frac12,\ldots,n_{\rm col}-\frac12,n_{\rm col}\right\},
\qquad
(S_a,S_b)=(s,s),
\]
where \(n_{\rm col}=d_{\rm R}\) in the current call pattern.

The first filter is exactly `CheckAmpConstruction`.  With right physical spins \(j_i\), right antispinor polarization \(p_i\), auxiliary antispinor polarization zero, and mass vector \(m_i\), the candidate
\[
(s,s,j_3,\ldots,j_n)
\]
is accepted only if
\[
d_{\rm R}\ge |s|+|s|+\sum_{i=3}^{n}|j_i|
\]
and the parity condition
\[
d_{\rm R}
+\sum_{\alpha=a,b,3,\ldots,n}
\begin{cases}
0, & m_\alpha=0,\ S_\alpha>0,\\
-2S_\alpha, & m_\alpha=0,\ S_\alpha\le 0,\\
p_\alpha, & m_\alpha\ne0
\end{cases}
-\left(2|s|+\sum_{i=3}^{n}|j_i|\right)
\]
is even.  It also requires the usual polarization bounds:
\[
p_\alpha=0
\quad\text{for massless legs,}
\qquad
0\le p_\alpha\le 2S_\alpha
\quad\text{for massive legs.}
\]
For the two auxiliary massless legs, this means \(p_a=p_b=0\).

After this filter, the amplitudes are generated by the usual SSYT construction.  Terms containing a massless self-column pair
\[
\{i,2n+1-i\}
\]
inside a single bracket are rejected before the formal \(J\) translation, because they would reduce to \(\langle ii\rangle\) or \([ii]\).  Then each allowed single auxiliary label in a bracket is either kept as a supplemental left label or replaced by \(J\), while any bracket containing both auxiliary labels is rejected.  Finally the sewn record must pass `Amp2MetaInfo`, allowed polarization-sector, nonzero reduction, and rank checks.

Thus \(S_a=S_b\) is the imposed representation-theory ansatz, while the magnitude \(s\) is not inferred from one tableau formula alone.  It is determined by the on-shell dimension and polarization constraints plus the subsequent formal-\(J\) sector checks.

## One-to-one issue

There is no literal one-to-one map from every auxiliary raw amplitude to a CF representative.  The auxiliary construction can generate redundant sewn records.  The meaningful statement is a span statement at the reduced massless-limit layer:
\[
\operatorname{Span}\{\overline A_{\rm sew}\}
{}=
\operatorname{Span}\{\overline A_{\rm CF}\}.
\]
The independent sewing output is obtained by row-reducing the sewing coefficient matrix on the same monomial basis as the CF block.  Therefore the basis dimensions agree after independence extraction, even when the raw sewing record count is larger than the CF record count.

## Dedicated left-spin-\(3/2\) systematic scan

A dedicated scan was then run with fixed left spin
\[
\ell=\frac32.
\]
The scan includes four-point cases with right spins
\[
\{1,1\},\quad \{1,0\},\quad \{0,1\},\quad \{2,1\},\quad \{1,2\},\quad \{2,0\},
\]
all allowed right mass choices for those spins, all right polarizations in the allowed massive-spin ranges, and
\[
d_{\rm amp}=5,6.
\]
It also includes representative five-point cases
\[
\{1,1,1\},\quad \{1,1,0\},\quad \{1,0,1\},\quad \{0,1,1\},\quad \{2,1,1\},\quad \{1,2,1\}
\]
at \(d_{\rm amp}=6\), plus representative six-point cases
\[
\{1,1,1,1\},\qquad \{1,1,1,0\}
\]
at \(d_{\rm amp}=6\).

The result was
\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{quantity} & \text{value}\\
\hline
\text{total cases} & 365\\
\text{compared cases} & 365\\
\text{complete cases} & 365\\
\text{nontrivial complete cases} & 114\\
\text{vacuous complete cases} & 251\\
\text{failed or timed out cases} & 0
\end{array}
\]
Thus, in this systematic \(\ell=\frac32\) scan there is again no counterexample to the reduced-span equality
\[
\operatorname{Span}\{\overline A_{\rm sew}\}
{}=
\operatorname{Span}\{\overline A_{\rm CF}\}.
\]
