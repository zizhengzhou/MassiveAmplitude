# Relative Completeness Proof for the Sewing Construction

## Status

This note formulates the sewing construction as a representation-theoretic
statement relative to the existing CF-block completeness theorem.  The goal is
not to reprove the full on-shell SSYT construction.  Instead, we assume the CF
basis is already known to be independent and complete, and then prove that the
left-right sewing construction is a reorganization of the same reduced
amplitude space, subject to one explicit right-residual lifting proposition.

The result is therefore conditional in the following precise sense:

1. The left three-point open basis and the symmetric sewing step have a direct
representation-theoretic proof.
2. For the direct two-row formal residual SSYT backend used by the construction,
there is a tableau-level equal-auxiliary-spin lift whenever the total number of
formal \(J\)-slots is even.  The sewing targets generated by the left basis have
this evenness property.
3. If the proof-level sewing construction is defined using the direct two-row
formal \(J\)-slot residual SSYT backend with the target filters stated below,
then the auxiliary lift is completely proven: every needed formal tableau has
an equal-spin two-auxiliary realization.
4. The stronger statement that the full auxiliary on-shell backend, treated as
a black-box generator, realizes every quotient-level formal residual class is
not needed for the proof-level construction.  It remains an implementation
equivalence theorem rather than a mathematical prerequisite for sewing
completeness.

The final theorem below is therefore stated for the proof-level construction:
left basis, direct formal \(J\)-slot residual SSYT, explicit equal-spin
auxiliary lift, and symmetric sewing.  A separate implementation theorem would
be needed only if one wants to prove that the full auxiliary on-shell backend
is equivalent to this proof-level backend as a black box.

## Ambient quotient space

Fix the external spins, masses, polarization sector, and bracket dimension
\(d\).  Let
\[
\mathcal P_d
\]
be the vector space of local Lorentz-covariant spinor polynomials in that
sector before quotienting by on-shell relations.  Let
\[
\mathcal I_{\rm os}
\]
be the subspace generated by the EoM relations, Schouten identities, momentum
conservation, and the massive-to-massless relabeling relations used in the
reduction pipeline.  The reduced amplitude space is
\[
\mathcal A_d=\mathcal P_d/\mathcal I_{\rm os}.
\]
All equalities of spans in this note are equalities in \(\mathcal A_d\).
We assume throughout that \(\mathcal I_{\rm os}\) is stable under the
heavy-pair \(SU(2)\) action.  This is necessary so that the \(J\)-channel
decomposition descends from \(\mathcal P_d\) to the quotient \(\mathcal A_d\).

The label \(J\) is used in the code-normalized convention of the construction:
it is an integer transmitted-current slot label.  A spin-\(J\) current carries
the symmetric slot structure used below, and the formulas count these integer
current slots directly.  If one rewrites everything in a convention where the
number of spinor slots is \(2J_{\rm phys}\), then all appearances of \(J\) in
slot counts should be replaced by the corresponding integer slot count.

## The CF theorem used as input

We assume the existing CF construction has already proved the following.

**Theorem 0, CF completeness.**  For the fixed sector above,
`ConstructIndepCFBlock` returns a set
\[
\mathcal C_d=\{C_\alpha\}
\]
whose images in \(\mathcal A_d\) are linearly independent and span
\(\mathcal A_d\):
\[
\operatorname{Span}\{\overline C_\alpha\}
{}=
\mathcal A_d .
\]

This theorem packages the hard global facts about the ordinary on-shell SSYT
basis: EoM quotienting, Schouten quotienting, momentum conservation, mass
assignments, polarization constraints, and the final independent-basis
selection.  The sewing proof below does not rederive these facts.

## Angular-momentum decomposition

Let the heavy pair on legs \(1,2\) have equal spin \(\ell\).  The heavy-pair
little-group representation is
\[
V_\ell\otimes V_\ell .
\]
Because finite-dimensional \(SU(2)\) representations over \(\mathbb C\) are
semisimple, every finite-dimensional subquotient of a polynomial space carrying
this action decomposes into isotypic components.  Since \(\mathcal I_{\rm os}\)
is assumed \(SU(2)\)-stable, the quotient also decomposes.  Therefore
\[
\mathcal A_d
{}=
\bigoplus_J \mathcal A_{d,J},
\]
where \(\mathcal A_{d,J}\) is the component in which the tensor indices
transmitted from the heavy pair to the right block transform as spin \(J\).

This is not a factorization pole statement.  It is the finite-dimensional
representation-theoretic statement that a local tensor polynomial can be
resolved into irreducible transmitted angular-momentum channels.

## Left three-point open space

For fixed \(J\), define the left open-current space
\[
\mathcal L_{\ell,J}
\]
to be the span of equal-spin three-object structures in which two on-shell
massive legs of spin \(\ell\) couple to one formal current slot of spin \(J\).
Let
\[
N=2\ell,\qquad m=\min(J,N),\qquad r=N-m .
\]
The proposed basis is
\[
\mathcal A^{(\ell)}_{J;a,b,k}
{}=
\langle1\cdot\rangle^a[1\cdot]^{m-a}
\langle2\cdot\rangle^b[2\cdot]^{m-b}
x^{r-k}y^k
\bigl(\langle Q\cdot\rangle[Q\cdot]\bigr)^{\max(J-N,0)},
\]
with
\[
a,b=0,\ldots,m,\qquad k=0,\ldots,r .
\]

**Lemma 1, left-basis completeness.**  Under the assumptions that the two heavy
legs are distinguishable, have equal spin \(\ell\), and no parity, charge
conjugation, or exchange projection has been imposed, assume moreover that:

1. The three-point static-recoil quotient has independent closed generators
\(x,y\).
2. Every closed heavy-pair scalar slot lies in the two-dimensional span of
\(x,y\).
3. The degree-\(r\) symmetric powers \(x^{r-k}y^k\) have no further relations in
the left three-point quotient.
4. \(\langle Q\cdot\rangle[Q\cdot]\) is the unique current-raising generator
for the excess \(J>N\) current slots.
5. No additional three-point Schouten or EoM relation mixes the open-slot,
closed-slot, and \(Q\)-raising factors.

Then the structures above form a basis of \(\mathcal L_{\ell,J}\).

**Proof.**  Each massive spin-\(\ell\) leg carries \(N=2\ell\) symmetric
little-group slots.  To couple to a spin-\(J\) current, at most \(m=\min(J,N)\)
slots on each heavy leg can be attached directly to current spinor slots.  On
leg \(1\), the \(m\) attached slots split into \(a\) angle brackets and
\(m-a\) square brackets.  This gives \(m+1\) independent symmetric choices.
The same argument gives \(m+1\) choices on leg \(2\).

The remaining \(r=N-m\) slots on each heavy leg are closed heavy-pair scalar
slots.  In the chosen static-recoil basis, each such pair lies in the
two-dimensional span of \(x,y\).  After symmetrization over the \(r\) identical
closed slots, the degree-\(r\) monomials
\[
x^{r-k}y^k,\qquad k=0,\ldots,r,
\]
form a basis of the closed part.

If \(J>N\), all heavy slots have already been attached to the current.  The
excess current spin is orbital and is generated by
\[
\langle Q\cdot\rangle[Q\cdot],
\]
one spin-one current-raising factor at a time.  Hence the required excess
factor is
\[
\bigl(\langle Q\cdot\rangle[Q\cdot]\bigr)^{J-N}.
\]

The three factors are independent tensor factors before imposing any extra
discrete symmetry.  Their products therefore form a basis.  The count is
\[
(m+1)^2(r+1),
\]
which agrees with the implemented construction.  This proves the lemma.

## Formal right residual space

For fixed \(J\), let
\[
\mathcal R_{J,d}
\]
be the quotient-level dual residual coefficient space for the
\(J\)-isotypic component.  More precisely, choose an \(SU(2)\)-equivariant
decomposition of \(\mathcal A_{d,J}\) relative to the complete left current
space \(\mathcal L_{\ell,J}\).  Then \(\mathcal R_{J,d}\) is the coefficient
space dual to \(\mathcal L_{\ell,J}\) in this decomposition, with the pairing
realized by formal \(J\)-slot contraction.

This definition includes the required nondegeneracy and surjectivity of the
left-right \(J\)-channel decomposition.  It is therefore a quotient-level
definition, not merely the set of all right-looking expressions that happen to
contract into \(\mathcal A_d\).

The formal \(J\)-slot SSYT rules give a tableau realization of
\(\mathcal R_{J,d}\).  This direct formal space is not the auxiliary on-shell
space.  It is the intended target of the auxiliary construction.

**Hypothesis F, formal residual completeness inherited from CF/SSYT.**  Assume
that the CF completeness theorem is obtained from the ordinary SSYT
construction in a way compatible with restriction to the transmitted
\(J\)-channel.  Equivalently, branching the CF tableau basis with respect to
the heavy-pair \(SU(2)\) action commutes with quotienting by
\(\mathcal I_{\rm os}\).  Then the direct formal \(J\)-slot SSYT rules give a
basis of the quotient-level residual dual coefficient space
\(\mathcal R_{J,d}\).

**Proof.**  The CF theorem gives a complete independent SSYT model of
\(\mathcal A_d\).  Since \(\mathcal I_{\rm os}\) is \(SU(2)\)-stable, the
quotient decomposes into \(J\)-isotypic components.  Ordinary semistandard
tableaux form a weight-compatible model for finite-dimensional polynomial
\(SU(2)\) representations; restricting a tableau to a fixed transmitted
\(J\)-type is therefore the usual branching operation.  After the complete
left current space \(\mathcal L_{\ell,J}\) is factored out, the remaining
coefficient tableaux are precisely the formal \(J\)-slot residual tableaux by
definition of the direct backend's alphabet and Hodge rules.  Thus these
direct formal tableaux span \(\mathcal R_{J,d}\).  Independence follows because
any relation among them would sew with the dual left basis to give a relation
inside the corresponding CF \(J\)-isotypic component, contradicting CF
independence.  Hence they form a basis.

This hypothesis is the exact point where the previous paper's CF/SSYT theorem
is used.  If the CF theorem already includes this branching compatibility,
then Hypothesis F is a corollary of that theorem.  The auxiliary particles are
not used to prove formal residual
completeness; they are only used to realize each formal residual tableau by an
ordinary on-shell auxiliary tableau when desired.

## Symmetric sewing

For each \(J\), define the sewing map
\[
\mu_J:
\mathcal L_{\ell,J}\otimes \mathcal R_{J,d}
\longrightarrow
\mathcal A_d
\]
by summing over all pairings of the matching \(J\)-slots.  In indices this is
\[
\mu_J(L\otimes R)
{}=
\sum_{\sigma\in S_{n_J}}
L^{I_1\cdots I_{n_J}}
R_{I_{\sigma(1)}\cdots I_{\sigma(n_J)}} ,
\]
with the analogous contraction applied separately in the angle and square
sectors of the bracket expression.

**Lemma 2, sewing realizes the \(J\)-channel pairing.**  The map \(\mu_J\) is
the invariant pairing between the spin-\(J\) current representation carried by
the left block and the dual formal \(J\)-slot representation carried by the
right block.  Its image is exactly the \(J\)-channel subspace
\(\mathcal A_{d,J}\), by the definition of \(\mathcal R_{J,d}\) as the full
quotient-level residual dual coefficient space.

**Proof.**  The current slots in \(\mathcal L_{\ell,J}\) transform as the
spin-\(J\) representation, and the formal \(J\)-slots in \(\mathcal R_{J,d}\)
transform as its dual.  The fully symmetric sum over all slot pairings is the
unique \(SU(2)\)-invariant contraction between these symmetric tensor
representations, up to normalization.  Normalization does not affect the span
in \(\mathcal A_d\).

By definition, \(\mathcal A_{d,J}\) consists of the reduced amplitudes whose
transmitted \(12\)-channel indices transform as spin \(J\).  Pairing a complete
left current space with the complete dual right residual space gives all such
invariants, and no invariant outside the \(J\)-isotypic component.  This proves
the claim.

## Auxiliary right residual construction

The implemented right construction uses two temporary massless auxiliary
particles \(a,b\) with equal spin
\[
S_a=S_b=s .
\]
For each allowed \(s\), the ordinary full on-shell SSYT constructor generates a
candidate auxiliary space.  A translation map
\[
\Phi_s:\mathcal R^{\rm aux}_{s,d}\longrightarrow \mathcal R_{J,d}
\]
is defined termwise:

1. An auxiliary-auxiliary bracket is rejected.
2. A massless self-pair column \(\{i,2n+1-i\}\) is rejected.
3. A single auxiliary occurrence paired with a physical right label may be
replaced by a formal \(J\)-slot.
4. An angular column consisting of the two auxiliary labels may be translated
to the supplemental heavy-pair column.
5. The result is retained only if its metadata and reduced amplitude match the
target sector.

Let
\[
\mathcal R^{\rm aux}_{J,d}
{}=
\sum_s \Phi_s\!\left(\mathcal R^{\rm aux}_{s,d}\right)
\subseteq
\mathcal R_{J,d}.
\]

The inclusion follows from the filters: every retained term has the required
formal \(J\)-slot count, bracket dimension, mass assignment, and polarization
metadata, and is then interpreted in the formal residual quotient.  This should
be understood as a well-definedness assumption on the translation map
\(\Phi_s\): translating an auxiliary representative and then reducing must give
the same class in \(\mathcal R_{J,d}\) for any auxiliary representatives that
are equivalent in the auxiliary on-shell quotient.  Metadata filters alone are
not a proof of this well-definedness.

The hard statement is the converse at the quotient level.  Before stating that
general proposition, we first prove the direct tableau lifting theorem that is
actually forced by the implemented two-row formal residual backend.

### Direct tableau lifting theorem

Let \(T\) be a two-row direct formal residual SSYT generated by
`SewingRightResidualRecordsDirect` for a sewing target.  Thus:

1. Rows are weakly increasing and columns are strictly increasing.
2. The formal alphabet is ordered as
\[
1<2<J<3<4<\cdots .
\]
3. The angular columns pass the formal \(J\)-Hodge validity test.
4. The tableau passes the target count filter inherited from the chosen left
record.
5. Supplemental labels \(1,2\) have zero target count on the square side.
6. Angular columns \(1;J\) and \(2;J\) are rejected by the formal \(J\)-Hodge
test.
7. A retained formal \(J\)-entry is required to have a physical partner after
the target filters are applied.

Let \(N_J(T)\) be the total number of formal \(J\)-entries in \(T\).

Let \(N_{12}^{\rm ang}(T)\) be the number of supplemental angular columns of
the form \(1;2\).  In the auxiliary lift below the common auxiliary slot count
is
\[
n_a(T)=n_b(T)
{}=
\frac{N_J(T)}{2}+N_{12}^{\rm ang}(T).
\]
In the package's physical spin convention this slot count is converted to the
massless auxiliary spin by the same spin-to-slot rule used by the ordinary
on-shell constructor.  The important point is not the name of the convention
but the equality \(n_a=n_b\).

**Lemma 3, evenness of sewing targets.**  For every right target produced from
a left three-point record, \(N_J(T)\) is even.

**Proof.**  Let \(N=2\ell\), \(m=\min(J,N)\), and
\(q=\max(J-N,0)\).  In every left basis element the direct heavy-current
attachments contribute \(m\) current slots from leg \(1\) and \(m\) current
slots from leg \(2\), hence \(2m\) formal \(J\)-slots in total.  The excess
current factor
\[
\bigl(\langle QJ\rangle[QJ]\bigr)^q
\]
contributes two more formal \(J\)-slots for each power.  Therefore the right
target must have
\[
N_J(T)
{}=
2m+2q
\]
formal \(J\)-entries in total.  This proves evenness.  The separate angle and
square counts can vary from one left basis element to another; only the total
count is used here.

**Lemma 4, position of direct formal \(J\)-entries.**  In an admissible direct
two-row formal residual tableau for the implemented sewing target, every
surviving \(J\)-entry is a top-row entry paired with a physical lower entry.

**Proof.**  Since columns are strictly increasing and
\[
1<2<J<3<4<\cdots ,
\]
a bottom-row \(J\) would have top entry \(1\) or \(2\).  In an angular column,
such a column is rejected by the explicit formal \(J\)-Hodge admissibility
rule: angular columns \(1;J\) and \(2;J\) are forbidden, and a \(J\)-column
must be paired with a physical label at least as large as the first right
physical label.  In a square column, a supplemental label paired with \(J\)
cannot satisfy the target counts for the right residual block, because
supplemental \(1,2\) labels are allowed only as angular supplemental columns.
Therefore every retained \(J\)-entry is in the top row, and its lower entry is
physical.

**Theorem R-direct, direct formal tableau lift.**  Let \(T\) be an admissible
direct two-row formal residual SSYT satisfying the seven target conditions
listed above and with even \(N_J(T)\).  Then \(T\) has an auxiliary SSYT lift
\(\widetilde T\) with equal auxiliary label multiplicities.  Under the
implemented translation rule, \(\widetilde T\) maps back to \(T\).

**Proof.**  Let the two auxiliary labels be \(a<b\), placed before the physical
right labels in the auxiliary alphabet.  In the code this is the local label
pair \(\{1,2\}\), distinct from the global heavy legs after translation.

By Lemma 4, every \(J\)-entry of \(T\) lies in the top row and is paired with a
physical lower entry.  Since \(T\) is semistandard, the \(J\)-entries form a
contiguous block in the weakly ordered top row.  By Lemma 3, their number is
even.  Replace the first half of these \(J\)-entries by \(a\), and the second
half by \(b\).  The auxiliary alphabet is
\[
a<b<\text{first physical right label}<\cdots .
\]
Thus all entries \(a,b\) are still smaller than the physical lower entries, so
column strictness is preserved.  The top row remains weakly ordered because
the replacement is made in the order \(a\le b\).  Supplemental formal labels
\(1,2\) do not occur in non-supplemental columns by the target filter; hence no
additional row-order obstruction is introduced by replacing the \(J\)-block.

Any angular supplemental column \(1;2\) of the formal tableau is lifted to the
auxiliary pair column \(a;b\).  This column is explicitly allowed only on the
angular side.  All other columns are left unchanged except for the \(J\)-to-\(a,b\)
replacement described above.

The two auxiliary labels now occur equally often: the split \(J\)-block
contributes \(N_J(T)/2\) copies of each label, and every lifted supplemental
angular column contributes one additional copy of each.  Therefore
\[
n_a(T)=n_b(T)
{}=
\frac{N_J(T)}{2}+N_{12}^{\rm ang}(T).
\]
Hence the lift has equal auxiliary spin data \(S_a=S_b=s(T)\), where \(s(T)\)
is the massless spin corresponding to this common slot count in the ordinary
on-shell filling convention.

Finally, the implemented translation sends a single auxiliary occurrence in a
non-supplemental column back to \(J\), and sends an angular \(a;b\) column back
to the supplemental \(1;2\) column.  Therefore the translated tableau is
exactly \(T\).  This proves the theorem.

### Gap resolution for the auxiliary construction

The possible gap was the following: using two equal-spin auxiliary particles
might fail to realize a formal right residual tableau whose \(J\)-slots cannot
be split evenly between the two auxiliary labels.

Theorem R-direct resolves this gap for the actual sewing construction.  The
reason is structural, not empirical:

1. A valid sewn left block always asks the right block for equal angle-type and
square-type \(J\)-slot counts, so the total number of right \(J\)-slots is
even.
2. In the admissible direct two-row backend, every \(J\)-entry that survives
the Hodge and target filters lies in the top row above a physical lower entry.
3. The whole \(J\)-block can therefore be split into an \(a\)-half and a
\(b\)-half without breaking semistandardness.
4. Each supplemental angular \(1;2\) column lifts to one \(a;b\) column, which
preserves equality of auxiliary multiplicities.

Thus the equal-spin convention \(S_a=S_b\) is not an arbitrary truncation in
the proof-level construction.  For every direct formal residual tableau that
can actually be sewn to a left \(\ell+\ell+J\) block, the equal-spin auxiliary
lift exists and its common auxiliary spin is fixed by the formula above.

The only tableaux excluded by equal-spin auxiliary lifting are odd-\(N_J\)
formal tableaux.  They do not appear in the sewing target because no left
\(\ell+\ell+J\) open-current structure supplies an odd total number of
right-contracted \(J\)-slots.

### Relation to the full auxiliary on-shell backend

The direct tableau theorem proves that every direct formal tableau in the
even-\(J\) sewing sector has an equal-spin auxiliary lift.  Together with the
definition that the retained auxiliary tableau backend is translated and keyed
by its formal image, this gives equality of retained formal tableau keys in
that sector.  It does not by itself prove that the full on-shell auxiliary
backend, after quotienting by EoM, Schouten identities, and momentum
conservation, realizes every quotient-level formal residual class.  That
stronger implementation statement is the following.

**Proposition R, quotient-level auxiliary realization.**  For every formal right
residual element
\[
R\in\mathcal R_{J,d},
\]
there exists a finite linear combination of auxiliary on-shell records, with
equal auxiliary spins \(S_a=S_b=s\) allowed to vary, whose translated image is
equal to \(R\) in the reduced quotient.  Equivalently,
\[
\mathcal R^{\rm aux}_{J,d}
{}=
\mathcal R_{J,d}
\quad\text{in}\quad \mathcal A_d .
\]

This proposition is not required for the proof-level sewing theorem below if
the right residual block is generated first as a direct formal \(J\)-slot SSYT
and then lifted explicitly to equal-spin auxiliary data using Theorem R-direct.
It is required only to prove that the current full auxiliary on-shell backend is
itself a complete black-box generator of the same quotient-level residual
space.

### Why this does not yet prove Proposition R

The full auxiliary implementation generates full on-shell amplitudes, not only
the direct residual formal tableaux, and then performs termwise translation and
reduction.  A complete proof of Proposition R must still address:

1. Quotient-level formal residual classes represented only after on-shell
relations are imposed.
2. Well-definedness of the auxiliary-to-formal map on auxiliary quotient
classes.
3. The absence of extra reduced directions after auxiliary translation.

The last point is controlled relative to CF completeness only after
well-definedness is established: if a translated auxiliary class is a valid
class in the same reduced amplitude quotient, then it cannot create a physical
direction outside \(\mathcal A_d\).  However, the exact equality
\(\mathcal R^{\rm aux}_{J,d}=\mathcal R_{J,d}\) still requires the lifting
surjectivity statement at the quotient level.

## Relative completeness theorem

**Theorem 1, proof-level relative sewing completeness.**  Assume:

1. Theorem 0, the CF independent-completeness theorem.
2. \(SU(2)\)-stability of the quotient ideal \(\mathcal I_{\rm os}\).
3. Existence of the nondegenerate equivariant left-right coefficient
decomposition used to define \(\mathcal R_{J,d}\).
4. Hypothesis F, direct formal residual SSYT completeness in each
\(J\)-channel.
5. The structural three-point assumptions of Lemma 1.
6. The invariant-pairing statement of Lemma 2.
7. The two-row direct-backend and even-sewing-target hypotheses used in
Theorem R-direct.

Use the proof-level sewing construction that:

1. enumerates the left basis \(\mathcal L_{\ell,J}\);
2. enumerates the direct formal \(J\)-slot residual SSYT basis of
\(\mathcal R_{J,d}\);
3. applies the explicit equal-spin two-auxiliary lift of Theorem R-direct to
realize each direct formal residual tableau when an auxiliary realization is
desired;
4. sums over all transmitted \(J\)-channels that occur in \(\mathcal A_d\); and
5. performs the fully symmetric \(J\)-slot sewing.

Then the resulting sewn amplitudes span the same reduced amplitude space as
the CF basis:
\[
\operatorname{Span}\{\overline A_{\rm sew}\}
{}=
\operatorname{Span}\{\overline C_\alpha\}
{}=
\mathcal A_d .
\]

**Proof.**  By \(SU(2)\) semisimplicity,
\[
\mathcal A_d=\bigoplus_J\mathcal A_{d,J}.
\]
By Lemma 1, the constructed left open basis spans the full
\(\mathcal L_{\ell,J}\) for each \(J\).  By Hypothesis F, the direct formal
\(J\)-slot SSYT backend spans the full residual dual coefficient space
\(\mathcal R_{J,d}\).  By Theorem R-direct, every direct formal residual
tableau in the even-\(J\) sewing sector has an equal-spin two-auxiliary lift,
so the auxiliary realization does not remove any direct formal residual basis
element.  By Lemma 2, sewing the complete left space with this complete right
residual space gives exactly \(\mathcal A_{d,J}\).  Summing over \(J\) gives
\[
\sum_J
\mu_J\!\left(\mathcal L_{\ell,J}\otimes \mathcal R_{J,d}\right)
{}=
\bigoplus_J \mathcal A_{d,J}
{}=
\mathcal A_d .
\]
By Theorem 0,
\[
\mathcal A_d=\operatorname{Span}\{\overline C_\alpha\}.
\]
Therefore the sewn amplitudes and the CF amplitudes span the same reduced
space.  Selecting any rank-maximal subset of sewn rows gives an independent
sewing basis of the same dimension as the CF basis.

## What is actually established now

The current mathematical status is:

1. The proof-level relative theorem is proven conditional on the
branching-compatible use of the CF/SSYT theorem stated in Hypothesis F.
2. Lemma 1 is proven only under the explicit three-point structural hypotheses
listed in the lemma.
3. Lemma 2 is a standard finite-dimensional representation-theory argument
once \(\mathcal R_{J,d}\) is defined as the full quotient-level residual dual
coefficient space and \(\mathcal I_{\rm os}\) is \(SU(2)\)-stable.
4. The direct two-row formal residual SSYT backend has an equal-spin
two-auxiliary tableau lift in the even-\(J\) sewing sector.
5. Proposition R remains open only as an implementation-equivalence theorem for
the full auxiliary on-shell backend treated as a black-box generator.
6. Existing rank scans verify the conclusion of Theorem 1 in many finite
sectors, including four-point, five-point, selected six-point, right spin \(2\),
and left spin \(\ell=3/2\) examples.

Thus the proof gap can be removed for the proof-level construction by using the
direct formal \(J\)-slot residual backend as primary and the auxiliary particles
as an explicit lift.  The only remaining gap concerns whether the current
implementation's full auxiliary on-shell backend is equivalent to that
proof-level backend without first passing through direct formal \(J\)-slot
tableaux.

## Suggested next proof target

The next target should be a standalone theorem:

**Theorem R-quot, implementation equivalence.**  The termwise
auxiliary-to-formal translation \(\Phi_s\)
descends to a well-defined map on the auxiliary on-shell quotient, and the sum
of its images over equal auxiliary spins \(S_a=S_b=s\) equals the formal
right-residual quotient space:
\[
\sum_s \operatorname{Im}\Phi_s
{}=
\mathcal R_{J,d}.
\]

This statement is exactly the missing bridge between the proof-level
construction and a proof that the current full auxiliary on-shell backend is a
complete black-box implementation of it.  The direct tableau lift theorem
already proves the corresponding pre-quotient statement for the direct two-row
formal backend in the even-\(J\) sewing sector.

## Independent mathematical audit

An independent mathematical audit was performed after the first draft of this
note.  The audit gave a conservative negative verdict: the conditional theorem is
formally correct only after the extra hypotheses now stated above are included,
and Proposition R remains open.  The audit specifically required:

1. An explicit convention for \(J\) as an integer current-slot label.
2. \(SU(2)\)-stability of the quotient ideal \(\mathcal I_{\rm os}\).
3. Extra left three-point hypotheses guaranteeing independence of \(x,y\), the
closed symmetric powers, and the \(Q\)-raising generator.
4. A quotient-level definition of \(\mathcal R_{J,d}\) as the full residual
dual coefficient space.
5. A clear statement that Proposition R and the quotient well-definedness of
\(\Phi_s\) are not yet proven in full generality.

A second independent mathematical review then sharpened the auxiliary lifting
claim.  It found no obstruction in the admissible sewing sectors inspected, but
it identified an exact obstruction to an overly broad Proposition R: a formal
tableau with an odd total number of \(J\)-slots cannot lift from equal
auxiliary spins.  The actual sewing targets avoid this obstruction because the
left open-current structures always require an even total number of right
